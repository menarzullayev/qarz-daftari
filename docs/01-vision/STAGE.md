# Stage Contract — 01 Vision

## Preconditions
Idea Selection is passed and project context exists.

## Inputs
Selected idea, project-config, evidence, relevant decisions.

## Agent task
Define mission, vision, long-term goals, product principles, and measurable strategic direction. Challenge vague claims and record material assumptions.

## Outputs
Completed `docs/01-vision/TEMPLATE.md` content in the project vision document.

## Human approval
Required for mission, strategic direction, target scope, or other high-impact product commitments.

## Handoff
Provide an explicit product direction for Problem Discovery and identify unresolved strategic questions.
