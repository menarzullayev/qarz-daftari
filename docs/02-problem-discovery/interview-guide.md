# Shopkeeper Interview Guide

Purpose: test the hypotheses in `OUTPUT.md` with 15 to 20 owners of mahalla grocery shops. Questions are in Uzbek because the interviews are; notes and analysis go back into the stage output in English.

## Ground rules

- Talk to the owner, in the shop, at a quiet hour. Ten to fifteen minutes.
- Do not describe the product until section 5. Earlier answers must not be shaped by it.
- Ask about what happened, not about what they would do. "Last month" beats "usually".
- Do not write down customers' names or any personal data about debtors.
- Record the district, shop size, and how long the shop has been open.

## 1. Opening (unprompted problems)

1. Do'konni necha yildan beri yuritasiz? Kuniga taxminan nechta xaridor keladi?
2. Do'kon yuritishda sizni eng ko'p qiynaydigan uchta narsa nima?

Note whether credit or debt is mentioned before you raise it. This is the test for EVID-012.

## 2. Credit practice

3. Nasiyaga mol berasizmi? Kimlarga berasiz, kimlarga yo'q?
4. Hozir taxminan nechta odam sizdan qarzdor? Jami summa qancha atrofida?
5. Bu summa bir haftalik savdongizning qancha qismiga teng?
6. Nasiyani qayerga yozasiz? Ko'rsata olasizmi? (Look at the format: amounts or product names, dates, signatures.)
7. Bitta nasiyani yozishga qancha vaqt ketadi? Navbat bo'lganda nima qilasiz?
8. Siz yo'qligingizda kim yozadi?

## 3. Repayment

9. O'tgan oyda berilgan nasiyaning qanchasi o'z vaqtida qaytdi?
10. Umuman qaytmagan qarzlar bo'ldimi? Oxirgi marta qachon va qancha?
11. Qarzni eslatish uchun nima qilasiz? Oxirgi marta qanday eslatgansiz?
12. Eslatish sizga noqulaymi? Mijoz xafa bo'lgan holat bo'lganmi?
13. Summa bo'yicha mijoz bilan tortishuv bo'lganmi?
14. Nasiya bermay qo'yishni o'ylab ko'rganmisiz? Nega bermay qo'ymaysiz?

## 4. Tools and channels

15. Telefonda qarz yozadigan ilova ishlatib ko'rganmisiz? Qaysi? Nega davom ettirmadingiz?
16. Telegram ishlatasizmi? Nasiyaga oladigan mijozlaringizning qanchasi Telegram ishlatadi deb o'ylaysiz?

## 5. Concept reaction

Describe in two sentences: the sale is recorded on the phone in a few seconds, the customer gets a Telegram message with the amount and confirms it, and a polite reminder goes out on the agreed day.

17. Bu sizga kerakmi? Nimasi yoqdi, nimasi yoqmadi?
18. Mijozlaringiz bunga qanday qaraydi deb o'ylaysiz?
19. Daftardan sekinroq bo'lsa ishlatarmidingiz?
20. Agar bu qarzlarning qaytishini yaxshilasa, oyiga qancha to'lashga tayyor bo'lardingiz?

## After each interview

Write down within the hour: the three problems named in question 2, the numbers from questions 4, 5, 9 and 10, the exact words used about reminders, and the answer to question 20. Bring the notes back to be recorded as evidence.
