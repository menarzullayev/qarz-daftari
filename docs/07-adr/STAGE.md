# Stage Contract — 07 ADR

## Preconditions
06 Architecture is PASSED.

## Inputs
Architecture, constraints, alternatives, evidence, existing decisions.

## Agent task
Capture important architectural and technical decisions as explicit records: problem, options, selected solution, rationale, consequences, and reversibility.

## Outputs
ADRs using `TEMPLATE.md` and linked Decision Log entries where material.

## Human approval
Required for high-impact or irreversible decisions; routine reversible decisions may be agent-approved.

## Handoff
Provide stable decisions and consequences for Technical Specification and Development Plan.
