# Stage Contract — 03 Market Research

## Preconditions
02 Problem Discovery is PASSED.

## Inputs
Problem statements, target users, use cases, existing research, assumptions.

## Agent task
Research market, competitors, substitutes, positioning, pricing, demand signals, risks, and relevant regulations. Use current reliable sources and record material evidence.

## Outputs
Completed market research document using `TEMPLATE.md`, with evidence and explicit uncertainty.

## Human approval
Required for major market positioning, pricing direction, or strategic market selection.

## Handoff
Provide evidence-backed market constraints and opportunities for PRD.
