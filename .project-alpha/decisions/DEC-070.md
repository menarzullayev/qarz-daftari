- Title: The service runs on one host reached through Cloudflare Tunnel, backed up to Cloudflare R2

- Chosen option: One host; Cloudflare Tunnel; encrypted daily backups to R2
- Rationale: The founder said the budget does not allow servers now. The tunnel needs no public address or open port, and R2 keeps a copy off the one disk, which the agent asked for after he first chose two locations on what were then two servers
