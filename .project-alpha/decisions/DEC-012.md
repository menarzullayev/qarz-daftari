# Decision Record

- Decision ID: DEC-012
- Stage: 04-prd
- Title: Change of direction: full product instead of pilot MVP
- Decision: Replace the pilot-MVP scope with a production-grade first release: (1) customers are notified and can dispute but are never asked to confirm entries; (2) credit entries carry itemized goods lines and a repayment date promised per sale; (3) multiple sellers per shop with professional role and permission handling; (4) pricing direction of 100,000 UZS a month; (5) the release target is production-ready, scalable and high quality, not a ten-shop pilot
- Context: After answering the interview guide himself (EVID-034), the founder chose these options through the question form on 2026-10-06, against agent recommendations on goods lines, on the pilot-MVP approach and on price. Demand, differentiation and willingness to pay remain unvalidated (EVID-012, EVID-013, EVID-019, EVID-020).
- Chosen option: Full production-grade product; notification with dispute only; itemized entries; multi-seller roles; 100,000 UZS a month
- Rationale: Founder decision based on his own expectations of how shops work (EVID-034). The agent records disagreement: this multiplies build effort before any field validation, removes the previously stated differentiation, and sets a price about four times the incumbent's entry tier (EVID-021) without evidence.
- Impact: High
- Reversibility: Medium
- Risk: HIGH
- Evidence: EVID-034, EVID-013, EVID-019, EVID-020, EVID-021
