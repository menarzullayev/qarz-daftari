# Decision Record

- Decision ID: DEC-007
- Stage: 06-architecture
- Title: Approve architecture direction
- Decision: Build the MVP as a chat-only Telegram bot implemented as a modular monolith with PostgreSQL, on a single virtual server hosted in Uzbekistan, with daily encrypted backups and recovery targets of four hours of downtime and at most 24 hours of lost entries
- Context: The PRD requires data hosted in Uzbekistan, chat-only core flows, an immutable ledger and a pilot of about ten shops operated by one person
- Chosen option: Single-server modular monolith, chat-only, hosted in Uzbekistan
- Rationale: Smallest design that meets every PRD requirement and can be run by one person; local hosting satisfies data localization (EVID-027) at modest cost (EVID-031); Telegram limits are far above pilot volume (EVID-032). Compliance of notifications passing through Telegram remains an open legal question (EVID-033)
- Impact: High
- Reversibility: Medium
- Risk: MEDIUM
- Evidence: EVID-027, EVID-031, EVID-032, EVID-033
- Approval required: yes
- Approval status: pending
