- Title: Who decides a subscription receipt from the review group
- Decision: The announcement in the review group carries the approve and reject buttons, and administrators in the group decide from there. The agent took this to mean platform administrators: a press is honoured only from someone on the allow-list with an active, confirmed account and a live administrator session, so ADR-017 holds in the group as elsewhere; any other member's press changes nothing. If the founder meant the Telegram group's own administrators, this must be revisited
- Context: Answered by the founder on 2026-10-07 when asked by the agent in the working session
- Chosen option: Buttons in the group, honoured for platform administrators only
- Rationale: The founder's words were that administrators in the group can approve and that a message without buttons will not do; this answers the specification's open question 5
