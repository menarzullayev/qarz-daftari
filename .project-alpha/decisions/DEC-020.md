- Title: Autonomous build and gate change
- Decision: The shopkeeper interviews and the hands-on pDaftar test no longer block the build before M4; they remain launch criterion 1. - Chosen option: Build does not stop for validation; launch does. Orchestrator with parallel helpers; agent decides and records.
